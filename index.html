Subscribe once and it stays up to date automatically.

  1. Tap the button above. It copies the calendar link and opens Google’s “From URL” page.

  4. Tap Add calendar. The events appear within a few hours.

Apple Calendar ›
  1. Tap the button above.
  2. A box will pop up asking to subscribe, tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Done. The events are now in your Calendar app.
